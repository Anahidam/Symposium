20 October 2026 · Biology Building B2|03, Room 109

Programme

A day of scientific talks, poster discussions, flash talks and awards, running from 10:00 to 17:00.

Programme not yet finalised: the exact number and length of talk and poster slots depends on how many contributions we receive, so the outline below is a provisional running order. A detailed, timed programme will be published on this page once registration closes.

10:00

Registration & Welcome Coffee

Check in, collect your badge and enjoy coffee before the day begins.

10:30

Opening Remarks

Welcome address by the organising committee and the Department of Biology.

10:45

Scientific Talks Talks

Selected talks by postdoctoral researchers and PhD students from across the biosciences. Number of talks and break times to be confirmed based on submissions.

12:30

Lunch & Poster Session Poster

Buffet lunch alongside an interactive poster session with discussion over coffee.

14:30

Scientific Talks & Flash Talks Flash

Further scientific talks alongside rapid-fire flash talk pitches from selected poster presenters.

16:30

Awards Ceremony Awards

Prizes for the best talk and best poster of the symposium.

17:00

Closing & Reception

Drinks and informal networking to close the day.
